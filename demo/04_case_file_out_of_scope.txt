IN THE COURT OF THE SPECIAL JUDGE (NDPS), BENGALURU

Bail Application No. 214/2025

The applicant, Suresh Kumar, was arrested on 2 February 2025 under Sections 20 and 29 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, after 1.2 kg of ganja was allegedly recovered from a parked scooter registered in his name. The applicant states that the scooter had been lent to a friend for the week, that he was not present at the time of the recovery, and that the quantity is below commercial quantity. He has been in custody for 90 days, the charge sheet has been filed, and he has no previous criminal record. The applicant prays to be released on bail on such terms as the court may think fit.
