LegalGPT demo: chat questions
Click "New chat" before each one. Mode: Legal. Law: Auto.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. EVERYDAY QUESTION (IPC, citations, judgment, "Why this answer?")
------------------------------------------------------------
Someone broke into my shed at night and stole my bicycle. Which offence is this and what is the punishment?

What it shows: no legal words in the question, yet it finds theft / theft in a building and house-breaking
by night (IPC 379, 380, 457 and related), cites each claim [n], shows the BNS number for each section,
cites a Supreme Court judgment, and explains itself in "Why this answer?".

------------------------------------------------------------
2. NEW CODE (BNS), because the events are after 1 July 2024
------------------------------------------------------------
On 12 August 2024 a man snatched a gold chain from a woman's neck on the road. What offence is this?

What it shows: answered under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. In our recorded run it cited BNS 303 (theft,
was IPC 379), BNS 309 (robbery, was IPC 392) and BNS 134 (was IPC 356); the exact sections can vary a
little between runs. Each BNS source shows the IPC section it replaced. Numbers come from the official
BPRD table, not from the AI.

4. CIVIL QUESTION (should be declined)
------------------------------------------------------------
My landlord is refusing to return my security deposit after I moved out. What can I do?

What it shows: "Out of scope", no IPC offence invented. Line to say: the same AI models answering alone
treated 47 to 90% of such questions as crimes; inside LegalGPT, zero.

5. EXPLAINABILITY AND BIAS (gender-sensitive: the bias check runs by itself)
------------------------------------------------------------
My husband Ramesh and his mother kept demanding more dowry and beat me, and I was found with burns six years after the marriage. What offences are these?

What it shows: "Understand this answer" opens with the bias check done (husband -> wife, Ramesh -> Rekha...),
the sections each version relied on, and each difference explained (required by law / variation /
possible bias). Click Caste or Income and run the check again: one sentence about the accused is added
and only that differs. Then How it decided -> Check the explanation, and Words that mattered. If Groq is slow, choose Gemini 3.5
flash lite first: the bias check makes about 4 model calls.
